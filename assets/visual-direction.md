# Visual direction

The profile uses an asymmetric personal-index layout: a small paper artwork beside the introduction, a restrained typographic hierarchy, plain project entries, quiet live statistics, and the existing numeric counter.

## Sources and adaptation

- [gc-minimal-zine-poster](https://github.com/LiamGvchi/gc-minimal-zine-poster/blob/main/SKILL.md): the main image recipe, generous open paper, a single small visual event, restrained serif type, and one jade accent.
- [photo-abstract-editorial](https://github.com/ZzzLc0405/photo-abstract-editorial/blob/main/SKILL.md): the relationship between a photographic fragment and sparse abstraction, a neutral ivory ground, and reduced color.
- [gathered-scenes-zine-skill](https://github.com/Zeejay0/gathered-scenes-zine-skill): the tactile seam between an image fragment and an abstract printed field.

The first skill supplies the theme-to-image workflow. The other two inform visual direction only: no user photograph was supplied, so no photo-preservation or scene-distillation workflow is claimed. Reference examples were inspected for visual grammar; their photographs, subjects, wording, and compositions were not reused.

## Artwork

- File: `zine-on-repeat.png`
- Title: *on repeat*
- New raster artwork generated for this profile; no custom decorative SVG.
- Recipe: upper-right fragment / cassette and unspooling tape / photo-to-abstract paper continuation / quiet serif / jade ink / fine paper scan.
- Interpretation: a small music object becomes a loop, echoing the developer's interest in music tools and repeated experiments.

## Final image-generation prompt

```text
Create one finished vertical 3:5 minimal independent-zine artwork, a raster image, intended as the small art insert beside a developer's GitHub profile. Full-frame cool ivory cotton paper, approximately #F5F3ED, with extremely fine paper fibers and a barely visible flatbed-scan grain; clean and fresh rather than brown or antique. No border or mockup. Let about 80% read as quiet open paper. Place one small off-axis visual event slightly above and to the right of center, occupying about 15% of the canvas. This is an intimate printed art page, never an advertising layout.

The theme is making small games, music tools, and web experiments: curiosity gives a small object another life. Express this through just one concrete relation: a tiny grayscale found-photograph-style clipping of an unbranded audio cassette, with a short ribbon of magnetic tape unspooling into one loose incomplete rectangular loop. The photograph-like cassette is the only object. At one edge of the clipping, the cassette's label continues across a narrow hand-torn fibrous paper seam as a single irregular jade-green printed strip; let one faint neutral contour echo the tape's direction. The clipping should feel physically printed and slightly xerox-soft, the abstract continuation should be spare and open. No existing source photograph is supplied: invent this new photographic fragment rather than reproducing any reference artwork.

Set the exact short phrase "on repeat" in a quiet old editorial serif, small, lower-left of the visual event, on the ivory paper. One phrase only, legible without becoming a headline; add no other words or fake archival metadata. Jade green #169B78 is the only chromatic ink, carried by the cassette label and its continuing paper strip. The green should stay clear and saturated, about 2% of the full page and around 20% of the visual cluster. Keep all other marks in restrained charcoal and grayscale. Subtle ink absorption and a very slight register imperfection unify photo, paper, type, and abstract strip.

Use flat orthographic scan lighting, matte paper, gentle contrast, generous irregular breathing room, and a contemplative playful mood. The small fragment should reward attention while remaining distinct at thumbnail size. Avoid full-bleed scenes, large text, brand logos, CTA copy, rounded UI cards, gradients, generic technology pictograms, decorative circles or grids, product-ad lighting, glossy 3D render, hard drop shadows, cartoon, anime characters, neon, dense collage, tape stickers, watermarks, copied reference subjects, and extra objects.
```
